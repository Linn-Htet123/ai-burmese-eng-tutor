Voice Pipeline & Cost Architecture

The R-TE-1 (<1s latency) and D-011 (item log from model) architecture · target = Row F, $2.61 per course

The R-TE-1 latency budget

  • • Sub-1s from end of learner speech to May's voice
  • • Retrieval practice under time pressure is the method
  • • Interrupt May within 300ms of new speech (R-TE-2)
  • • Per-stage context reset avoids 30-min accumulated audio re-bills
  • • Reconnect must NOT resend prior audio (R-TE-5)

D-011 · The item-log lever

  • • Live model emits structured JSON alongside audio
  • • Removes the separate transcription stream
  • • Roughly 45% of optimised session cost
  • • Takes course from $4.78 (Row D) → $2.61 (Row F)
  • • PROTOTYPE IN WEEK 1 · reliability is the whole bet

What must be persisted

  • • Item log rows (spaced review queue)
  • • Session audio (learner-only ACL, R-TE-10)
  • • Fluency metrics per session (R-PR-5)
  • • Analytics events, session-number granular
  • • Billed tokens per session per stage from day one (R-TE-8)
