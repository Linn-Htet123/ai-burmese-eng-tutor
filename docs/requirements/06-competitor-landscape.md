## A. Closest competitors — global AI voice tutors

### 1. Speak — speak.com
The market leader (~$100M+ raised, born in Korea). Structured curriculum with embedded open AI voice conversation; voices cloned from native speakers.
**vs ours:** UI/scaffolding in Korean, Japanese, Spanish etc. — **no Burmese**. Infinite subscription, no course deadline, no human-reviewed placement, requires app install and an international card.

### 2. Loora
English-only AI voice tutor: open topics plus scenario practice (job interviews, meetings, presentations), instant feedback. Closest *product shape* to ours.
**vs ours:** full English immersion — a level-3 Burmese learner drowns. No L1 explanations, no deadline structure, no before/after proof, card-only payment.

### 3. ELI (elispeak)
Scenario reps with measurable progress scores; 15 free minutes/day.
**vs ours:** scenario drills, not a taught course. No Burmese, no upgrade-loop mechanic (offer better phrase → learner must say it back).

### 4. EnglishCall AI
Phone-call-style, audio-only practice — closest to our "just talk" feel.
**vs ours:** no curriculum, no L1 support, no progression system.

### 5. TalkPal / Talkio / Kippy / SpeakShark (the GPT-wrapper crowd)
Many-language conversation apps, text + voice, cheap or freemium.
**vs ours:** generic chat with a bot, not a structured 30-minute lesson. Voice is typically turn-based with 2–4s latency, not live. Zero Burmese scaffolding.

## B. Pronunciation specialists

### 6. ELSA Speak
The pronunciation app (Vietnamese founder, strong SEA presence). Scores individual sounds, stress, rhythm. ~$12/month.
**vs ours:** learners read scripted prompts — ELSA itself does not build conversational fluency. A complement, not a competitor; some of our target users may already have it.

### 7. BoldVoice
Hollywood accent-coach videos + AI feedback.
**vs ours:** accent polish for people who already speak; our users need fluency first.

## C. Avatar / gamified

### 8. Praktika
Animated 3D avatar tutors, scenario-based, heavy marketing spend.
**vs ours:** avatar novelty over pedagogy. No L1 support, no course structure, no accountability.

### 9. Duolingo (Max / "video call with Lily")
Gamified lessons; AI video-call practice on the top tier.
**vs ours:** speaking is a side feature on a streak game, famously weak for real conversation. No Burmese-based English course either.

## D. Human tutoring (the honest price anchor)

### 10. italki / Cambly / Preply
Marketplaces for live human tutors, $8–25/hour.
**vs ours:** this is what the BRD prices against — "twelve live sessions with feedback" at a fraction of tutor cost. Their weaknesses: scheduling friction, tutor-quality lottery, cost, and tutors don't speak Burmese.

## E. Myanmar-local (our home turf)

### 11. SAYA — saya.education
Myanmar's serious player: recorded lectures from top local teachers, A1–C1, all four skills.
**vs ours:** recorded video + exercises — **no live AI voice conversation at all**. They own "learn English" mindshare locally; we own "actually speak."

### 12. "English Speaking For MM" and similar Play Store apps
Phrasebooks with Burmese translations, offline audio. Huge install counts, free.
**vs ours:** static content, zero interaction. They prove local demand exists; they don't compete on outcome.

## F. Adjacent / watchlist

### 13. Stimuler
India-focused AI speaking coach (IELTS-leaning), voice notes + feedback. The "emerging-market, exam-driven" playbook. *(From prior knowledge, not this research pass — verify before citing externally.)*

### 14. Gliglish
Browser-based AI speaking teacher — notable as the rare **web-first** product like us. Thin curriculum.

## What nobody on this list has (our differentiators, per our own docs)

1. **Burmese-first scaffolding that changes with level** (R-LV-4: the instruction-language ratio shifts as the learner improves). No global app does L1-ratio pedagogy for Burmese.
2. **The upgrade loop with forced say-it-back** (D-004). Apps *correct* learners; none make the learner *use* the better phrase before the session ends.
3. **A pack-shaped first month inside a subscription** (D-042 + D-043) — daily 40-min practice with a week-4 before/after clip as the completion milestone. Every competitor is either a pure infinite subscription (no completion moment) or a one-off course (no daily habit). We are the only product that gets *both* the deadline psychology and the daily habit.
4. **Before/after voice proof** (R-PR-2). Nobody records day 1 to play against the week-4 clip.
5. **Payment without an international card** (R-PY-1). Every global app dies at the Myanmar checkout — this alone removes them from our market.

## Watch closely

- **Speak** — if they ever add Burmese scaffolding, they are the threat.
- **SAYA** — if they bolt an AI voice tutor onto their local brand and content.
- **ELSA** — SEA distribution muscle, could widen from pronunciation to conversation.
